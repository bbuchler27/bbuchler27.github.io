Cybersecurity Research · Industry Analysis

Cybersecurity Mini Almanac

An industry research project exploring the cybersecurity landscape, company activity, and market developments.

Project Overview

During my summer internship at Momentum Cyber, I contributed to cybersecurity industry research and analysis. My work involved examining companies, industry developments, and market activity to help organize and communicate relevant information.

The Cybersecurity Mini Almanac showcases research related to the broader cybersecurity ecosystem and demonstrates how complex industry information can be presented in an accessible format.

The Business Question

How can cybersecurity market information be organized and analyzed to help readers better understand the industry, identify relevant developments, and evaluate market activity?

This project focused on turning research into structured information that could support informed business discussions.

My Role & Responsibilities

As a Summer Research Associate at Momentum Cyber, I supported cybersecurity company research, market analysis, and transaction-related research.

My responsibilities included maintaining research databases, reviewing company information, organizing findings, and contributing to research materials and reporting templates.

This experience strengthened my ability to investigate unfamiliar companies, work with industry information, and communicate findings clearly and accurately.

Research Approach

My research process emphasized gathering relevant company and industry information, organizing findings consistently, and presenting insights in a format that readers could understand.

Working with cybersecurity market information required attention to detail, careful categorization, and an understanding of how different businesses operate within the industry.

Challenges & Lessons Learned

One of the challenges of cybersecurity research is the breadth of the industry. Companies often operate across multiple categories, making consistent classification and comparison important.

I learned how to approach unfamiliar technical subjects, evaluate research information, and translate complex material into clear, business-focused communication.

Business Value

Organized industry research can help analysts and decision-makers understand market trends, assess company positioning, and identify relevant developments.

This project demonstrates my ability to combine analytical thinking, industry research, and professional communication in a fast-changing business environment.
